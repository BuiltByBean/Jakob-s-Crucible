### 🎙️ INTRODUCING The Wisdom Crucible with Jakob McClain

**Greetings!**
My name is **Jakob — McClain.**

I’m a **Doctor of Physical Therapy** by my formal training —
but I’m a **lifelong student of the Bible**
by a **deeper** and **greater** conviction.

Long before I ever studied at Baylor,
I was learning through faithful teachers and independent studies.

And in recent months,
my passion for studying and explaining **God’s Word** has grown
such that I could gladly devote myself
to a full-time teaching ministry —
while still using my clinical skills as the Lord provides opportunity.

My goal for the recordings that I have planned next
is to take the speaking notes I use when leading our church’s adult Bible study
and expand them into a more thorough exploration of **Scripture**.

For those watching my corresponding videos on YouTube,
I’ll also display the relevant text on screen so that you can follow along
even if you don’t have your own copy of God’s Word in front of you.

My preferred method of studying the Bible
is to proceed **verse-by-verse** through a passage —
drawing connections to other passages,
using one Scripture to clarify the truth of another.
But I also perform word studies when differences in interpretation
**hinge on how a particular word is translated**,
and I occasionally survey Scripture
to discover everything that has been written about a specific topic.

My approach is **primarily exegetical** —
I look to Scripture to reveal truth,
even, and especially, when it challenges me.

But I also draw from the **macro-structure**
or **narrative architecture** of a passage at times
to create memorable and relatable illustrations.

My communicative posture may seem oriented
to **well-read, Bible-believing Christians** —
but I’ll also aim to explain with clarity and evidence
so that even if you’re new to the Bible,
you can still follow the narrative and reasoning of Scripture
and my occasional figurative linguistic constructions —
analogies, metaphors, and such.

I’ll include links to my **personal** study notes in the **show-notes**
for anyone who wishes to **critique** them, **study** them… or hang them on your wall.
Once you download them, they’re yours.
Do what you want with them.

From my **studies** **of** and **prayers through** the book of Proverbs,
I’ve come to understand that **acquiring wisdom**
is not **merely** an accumulation of knowledge —
but a **forging** of understanding —

**refined** by truth according to God’s design.

My **hope** and **prayer** are that the Lord will bless this process
as I work through it —
for the purpose of **encouraging** and **equipping** me as I look to Him,
and those who follow after me;

**not** to produce followers of Jakob McClain,
but to produce **disciples of Jesus Christ.**

Whether you’re deeply **interested**,
or simply looking to satisfy your skeptical **curiosity**,
I **humbly** invite you proceed from this introduction
to discover what the Bible has to say **to you**.

You can follow this podcast
**or** search for **The Wisdom Crucible** on YouTube for video recordings.

Dollar signs and media metrics drive the algorithms,
but I’m not here for those things.

**Rather**, my desire to discuss the Bible
is motivated by the hope of multiplying
the number of **treasures being stored up in Heaven**:
the **souls saved — by grace — through faith —**
in the **redemptive sacrifice** of **Jesus Christ** on the cross,
which satisfies the **justice** of our **awesome** and **loving** **Creator**.
