### ANALOGY

All people exist as portions of an ocean. Using the free will that God gave you, allowing Him his due honor as the first and over-arching priority of your life, invites His Spirit to fall on you as rain. This initiates a wave rippling in every direction, revealing expanding domains of influence as God propagates the impact of your relationship with Him.

For a married person, the first domain includes the two that became one, then the kids, the extended family, friends, coworkers, etcetera. Your influence is strongest within your wingspan; close to your core. The farther out you reach, the more strained and off-balance you become.

God’s reach, however, is unlimited. He is the rain that reaches to every corner of the world. As the rain falls on each portion of the ocean, some take little notice as they have sunk below the surface — into the darker depths. However, even the darkest depths are susceptible to the unseen three-dimensional ripples when the impact is sufficiently strong, or the surface yields to its influence.

### WRAPPING IT UP

Prioritize God over and above everything and everyone in your life, without exception, and you will be amazed as you witness every other responsibility and relationship fall into place.

There may be people that seem outside of your sphere of influence but if you deal rightly with those within your reach, and they do the same, an act of love can reach around the world.
